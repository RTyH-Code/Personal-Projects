## Fossils

Smashing rocks can turn up: Helix Fossil, Dome Fossil, Old Amber, Skull Fossil, Armor Fossil, Claw Fossil, Root Fossil. The scientist in the Ruins of Alph Research Center revives them at once;
the Pewter Museum still does after the Elite Four.
