# Playing HeartGold QoL + Hard

A short guide for a first run. It has no story spoilers beyond what bosses you will meet; the
[spoiler guide](README.md) has everything else (where each Pokémon is, every boss team, every item).

## Before you start

- Patch a US HeartGold ROM and start a new game (see the [README](../README.md)). Old saves won't work.
- Text is Fast by default and battles run at 60 fps. Running Shoes work from the start (hold B).
- **Optional challenge rules:** the woman downstairs in your house keeps a level cap (at the strongest level
  on the next boss's team; Rare Candies stop there too), a no-items rule for trainer battles, and a
  party-wide Exp. Share (the Pokémon that didn't battle get half the experience). All start off; talk to her
  again any time to change them. For Set mode, choose Battle Style: Set in Options.
- The Pokédex is in National mode from the beginning. A few extras the original unlocks with the National
  Dex (the Hoenn and Sinnoh radio, the wider Bug-Catching Contest, the Supreme Cup) open when you become
  Champion instead.

## What plays differently

- **Bosses are much tougher.** Every Gym Leader, the Elite Four, your rival, the Team Rocket executives and
  Red have new teams with held items and real movesets; from Chuck on they also have natures and trained
  stats. Expect to switch, heal and pick type matchups.
- **Experience is doubled and scales with level.** A team of six that you rotate through the story, beating
  the trainers you pass, stays within about three levels of each boss without grinding. Beating a Pokémon
  well above yours gives much more; one well below gives little.
- **Wild Pokémon are a little under the next boss** on the main path, side areas and the water, so a short
  grind catches you up quickly. Fishing, Rock Smash and Headbutt follow the same bands.
- **Every Pokémon family can be caught in Johto before the Elite Four**, with nothing needing a trade, the
  Day Care or the Safari Zone. Trade evolutions use an item (King's Rock, Metal Coat and the like) or
  happen at Lv. 37. The Safari Zone is still there but has nothing you can't get elsewhere.
- **HMs work without being taught**, as long as the HM is in your bag and you have the badge. HM moves can
  be forgotten any time. TMs are reusable.
- **Friendship evolutions** happen at 160 friendship instead of 220.
- **Breeding is quicker:** eggs hatch in half the steps, and two Pokémon that can breed always have an egg
  waiting when the Day Care checks, every 256 steps.
- **Moves can be remembered for free:** a woman in Cherrygrove City's Pokémon Center, and the Move Maniac in
  Blackthorn City, make a Pokémon remember any move it could have learned by level, as often as you like.

## Shops

- **Poké Marts** stock better items a badge sooner than the original: Great Balls and Revives at two badges,
  Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions and Full Heals at four, Max Potions at six, Full Restores at eight.
- **Goldenrod Department Store 3F:** Oran and the status berries from the start; Sitrus Berry, Lum Berry
  and Ether at four badges; the type-resist berries and Elixir at six; Max Ether, Max Elixir and Max Revive
  at eight. The Lucky Egg is here from four badges.
- **Goldenrod Department Store 4F:** the vitamins, and every evolution item, in tiers:

  | Badges | Items | Price |
  |---|---|---|
  | From the start | Moon, Sun and Oval Stones, Deep Sea Tooth and Scale | ₽3,000 |
  | 4 | Fire, Water, Thunder, Leaf and Ice Stones, King's Rock, Up-Grade, Prism Scale | ₽3,000 |
  | 6 | Metal Coat, Dragon Scale, Dusk, Dawn and Shiny Stones; Black Augurite | ₽3,000; ₽5,000 |
  | 7 | Peat Block, Protector, Electirizer, Magmarizer, Dubious Disc, Reaper Cloth, Razor Claw, Razor Fang | ₽5,000 |

- **Goldenrod Department Store, training items:** the EV-lowering berries on 3F from the start (₽500); the
  Power items on 4F from four badges (₽5,000); on 5F the nature mints from four badges (₽5,000), and the Bottle
  Cap (₽20,000) and Gold Bottle Cap (₽60,000) from eight. The man by the 5F counter takes a Bottle Cap to make
  the IV you pick 31, or a Gold Bottle Cap for all six.
- **Celadon Department Store 4F** (after the Elite Four): Leftovers, the Choice items, Life Orb and Focus
  Sash (₽25,000), other held items (₽10,000), Rare Candies (₽3,000), the Ability Capsule and Ability Patch.

## Levels to aim for

"You arrive at" is what a team of six reaches by beating every trainer on the way and nothing else. "Won
at" is the level at which an automated playtester, healing and switching like a player with one fixed
team, beat the boss within five tries; a player choosing a team for each boss will do better. A short grind
in the wild areas just before a boss closes most gaps.

| Bugsy | 16-19 | 14-16 | 14 |

| Morty | 26-29 | 25 | 25 |
| Chuck | 31-34 | 31-32 | 34 (not 32); his Poliwrath and Heracross want Flying or Psychic |
| Jasmine | 35-38 | 32-33 | 32 |
| Petrel, Rocket Hideout | 36-39 | 35 | 36 (not 32) |
| Pryce | 38-42 | 37-38 | 37 |
| Radio Tower (Proton to Archer) | 41-46 | 39-44 | 42 |
| Clair | 44-47 | 45-46 | 45 |

### After the Elite Four

| Boss | Their levels | You arrive at | Won at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kanto Gym Leaders | 61-72 | 56-71 | 62-73, about their average; grind a little before Lt. Surge |
| Blue | 73-76 | 72 | 74 |
| Will, Koga (second round) | 77-81 | 73 | 78-79 |
| Bruno, Karen (second round) | 79-84 | 74-75 | 82 and 84, their top levels |
| Lance (second round) | 85-88 | 75-76 | 95 (not 88): still a very hard fight |
| Red | 92-95 | 76-77 | 93 |

The second round and Red are a long grind (about 280 and 300 more wild battles); Mt. Silver is the quickest
place for it, and the Rare Candies in Celadon are there for anyone who'd rather pay than grind. The Gym
Leader rematches (Lv. 75-86) and the rival's later fights fall to a team at about their top level; Clair's
rematch is the toughest of them.

More detail on all of this: [the level curve](../playtest/LEVEL_CURVE.md) and
[the playtest results](../playtest/BALANCE.md).
