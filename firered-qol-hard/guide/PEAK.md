# Peak trainer prep

Every post-game peak team (all Lv. 100), with each Pokémon's real stats, what the team attacks with, what it's weak to, and Pokémon that match up well. Gym Leaders rematch any number of times once you're Champion, and each win gives a Nugget (35%), PP Up (25%), PP Max (15%) or Star Piece (25%); the Elite Four and Champion use these teams when the League official in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center has the PEAK challenge switched on. Each peak Champion win gives a Master Ball (20%), 3 PP Max (30%), 3 Star Pieces (25%) or 5 Nuggets (25%). In Nuzlocke mode these are exhibition battles: Pokémon that faint aren't lost (they're left with 1 HP), and losing doesn't end the run.

How peak Pokémon are built: perfect IVs; 252 EVs in the attacking stat their moves use; 252 Speed EVs (or 252 HP for Pokémon with base Speed under 50); Jolly/Timid if base Speed is 80+, otherwise Adamant/Modest. Their held items are listed below, and each trainer also carries Full Restores.

**Suggested counters** are picked automatically from fully evolved, non-legendary Pokémon (base stat total 470+, Attack or Sp. Atk 80+): they score for hitting team members super effectively with their own types and for resisting the team's attacks, counting either ability (the nature and ability coach can swap it). Treat them as a starting point; each links to its Pokédex page, which says where to catch it.
