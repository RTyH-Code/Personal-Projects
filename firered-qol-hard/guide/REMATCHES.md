# VS Seeker rematches

Use the VS Seeker on a route and the trainers there who want a rematch raise their hands. Each trainer's team gets stronger as you progress; the table lists every version and when it unlocks. You can fight the newest one each time the VS Seeker recharges (100 steps).

**Catch-up:** until you have all eight badges, any rematch Pokémon more than 5 levels under the next Gym Leader's strongest is raised to that level and evolves if it can, so rematches stay worth fighting even when the listed team is lower.

The Victory Road rematches are listed in [Trainers](TRAINERS.md#victory-road-rematches-vs-seeker).
