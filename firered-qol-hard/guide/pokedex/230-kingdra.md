# #230 Kingdra

*The Dragon Pokémon* — **Water / Dragon**

> It sleeps deep on the ocean floor to build its energy. It is said to cause tornadoes as it wakes.

Height 1.8 m (5′11″) · Weight 152 kg (335.1 lbs)

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
