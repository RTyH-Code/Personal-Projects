# #899 Wyrdeer

*The Big Horn Pokémon* — **Normal / Psychic**

> The black orbs on its antlers shine with an eerie light. It creates a barrier with psychic power.

Height 1.8 m (5′11″) · Weight 95.1 kg (209.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Frisk**: Checks the foe's item.
- **Sap Sipper** *(hidden ability)*: Grass moves raise Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
