# #313 Volbeat

*The Firefly Pokémon* — **Bug**

> It lives around clean ponds. At night, its rear lights up. It converses with others by flashing its light.

Height 0.7 m (2′04″) · Weight 17.7 kg (39.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Illuminate**: Encounter rate increases.
- **Swarm**: Ups Bug moves in a pinch.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

- **Route 15**: Grass / cave Lv 26 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Volbeat at the Four Island Day Care
