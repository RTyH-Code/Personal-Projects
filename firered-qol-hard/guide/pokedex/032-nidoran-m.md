# #032 Nidoran♂

*The Poison Pin Pokémon* — **Poison**

> Its large ears are flapped like wings when it is listening to distant sounds. It extends toxic barbs when angered.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 9 kg (19.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Poison Point**: Poisons foe on contact.
- **Rivalry**: Strong vs. the same gender.
- **Hustle** *(hidden ability)*: Trades accuracy for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 3**: Grass / cave Lv 6–7 (14%)
- **Safari Zone Center**: Grass / cave Lv 22 (20%)
- **Safari Zone East**: Grass / cave Lv 24 (20%)
- **Safari Zone North**: Grass / cave Lv 30 (20%)
- **Safari Zone West**: Grass / cave Lv 22 (20%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Nidoran♂ or Nidorino or Nidoking at the Four Island Day Care
