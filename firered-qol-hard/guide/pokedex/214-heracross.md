# #214 Heracross

*The Single Horn Pokémon* — **Bug / Fighting**

> Usually docile, but if disturbed while sipping honey, it chases off the intruder with its horn.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 54 kg (119.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swarm**: Ups Bug moves in a pinch.
- **Guts**: Ups Attack if suffering.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*

## Evolution

Heracross does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Six Island Pattern Bush**: Grass / cave Lv 15–30 (15%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Heracross at the Four Island Day Care
