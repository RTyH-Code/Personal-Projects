# #119 Seaking

*The Goldfish Pokémon* — **Water**

> The horn on its head is sharp like a drill. It bores a hole in a boulder to make its nest.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 39 kg (86.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Water Veil**: Prevents burns.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
