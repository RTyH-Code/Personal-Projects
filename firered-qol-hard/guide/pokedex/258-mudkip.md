# #258 Mudkip

*The Mud Fish Pokémon* — **Water**

> Its large tail fin propels it through water with powerful acceleration. It is strong in spite of its size.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 7.6 kg (16.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Torrent**: Ups Water moves in a pinch.

- **Six Island Ruin Valley**: Grass / cave Lv 15–18 (4%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Mudkip or Marshtomp or Swampert at the Four Island Day Care
