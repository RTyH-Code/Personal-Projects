# #141 Kabutops

*The Shellfish Pokémon* — **Rock / Water**

> It swims freely through water. It catches prey with its scythe-like arms and drains the victim's fluids.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 40.5 kg (89.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Battle Armor**: Blocks critical hits.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
