# #130 Gyarados

*The Atrocious Pokémon* — **Water / Flying**

> It has an extremely aggressive nature. The Hyper Beam it shoots from its mouth totally incinerates all targets.

Height 6.5 m (21′04″) · Weight 235 kg (518.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Moxie** *(hidden ability)*: KOs raise Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
