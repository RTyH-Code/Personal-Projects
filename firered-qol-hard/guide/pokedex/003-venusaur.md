# #003 Venusaur

*The Seed Pokémon* — **Grass / Poison**

> A bewitching aroma wafts from its flower. The fragrance becalms those engaged in a battle.

Height 2 m (6′07″) · Weight 100 kg (220.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Overgrow**: Ups Grass moves in a pinch.
- **Chlorophyll** *(hidden ability)*: Raises Speed in sunshine.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
