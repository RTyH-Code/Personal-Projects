# #316 Gulpin

*The Stomach Pokémon* — **Poison**

> There is nothing its stomach can't digest. While it is digesting, vile, overpowering gases are expelled.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 10.3 kg (22.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Liquid Ooze**: Draining causes injury.
- **Sticky Hold**: Prevents item theft.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
