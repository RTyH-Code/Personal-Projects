# #128 Tauros

*The Wild Bull Pokémon* — **Normal**

> When it is about to Tackle, it whips its body repeatedly with its three long tails.

Height 1.4 m (4′07″) · Weight 88.4 kg (194.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Anger Point**: Critical hits max Attack.
- **Sheer Force** *(hidden ability)*: Drops added effects for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Safari Zone North**: Grass / cave Lv 28 (1%)
- **Safari Zone West**: Grass / cave Lv 25 (4%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Tauros at the Four Island Day Care
