# #338 Solrock

*The Meteorite Pokémon* — **Rock / Psychic**

> It absorbs solar energy during the day. Always expressionless, it can sense what its foe is thinking.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 154 kg (339.5 lbs)

Solrock does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Mt. Moon B2F**: Grass / cave Lv 11 (4%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Solrock at the Four Island Day Care
