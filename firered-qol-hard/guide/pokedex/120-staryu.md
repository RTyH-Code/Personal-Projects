# #120 Staryu

*The Star Shape Pokémon* — **Water**

> It appears in large numbers by seashores. At night, its central core flashes with a red light.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 34.5 kg (76.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Illuminate**: Encounter rate increases.
- **Natural Cure**: Heals upon switching out.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

- **Route 21 North**: Super Rod Lv 15–25 (40%)
