# #190 Aipom

*The Long Tail Pokémon* — **Normal**

> It lives atop tall trees. When leaping from branch to branch, it deftly uses its tail for balance.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 11.5 kg (25.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.
- **Pickup**: May pick up items.
- **Skill Link** *(hidden ability)*: Multi-hit moves hit 5 times.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

- **Six Island Altering Cave**: Grass / cave Lv 20–24 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Aipom or Ambipom at the Four Island Day Care
