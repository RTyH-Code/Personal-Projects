# #211 Qwilfish

*The Balloon Pokémon* — **Water / Poison**

> The small spikes covering its body developed from scales. They inject a toxin that causes fainting.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 3.9 kg (8.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Poison Point**: Poisons foe on contact.
- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Intimidate** *(hidden ability)*: Lowers the foe's Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
