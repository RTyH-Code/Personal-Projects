# #050 Diglett

*The Mole Pokémon* — **Ground**

> It burrows through the ground at a shallow depth. It leaves raised earth in its wake, making it easy to spot.

Height 0.2 m (0′08″) · Weight 0.8 kg (1.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Sand Veil**: Ups evasion in a sandstorm.
- **Arena Trap**: Prevents fleeing.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
