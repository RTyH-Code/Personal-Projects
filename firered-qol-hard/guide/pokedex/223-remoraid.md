# #223 Remoraid

*The Jet Pokémon* — **Water**

> Using its dorsal fin as a suction pad, it clings to a Mantine's underside to scavenge for leftovers.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 12 kg (26.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hustle**: Trades accuracy for power.
- **Sniper**: Powers up critical hits.
- **Moody** *(hidden ability)*: Stats change every turn.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

- **Vermilion City**: Super Rod Lv 25–35 (4%)
- **Route 21 South**: Super Rod Lv 15–25 (40%)
