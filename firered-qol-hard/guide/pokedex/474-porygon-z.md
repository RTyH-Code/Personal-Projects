# #474 Porygon-Z

*The Virtual Pokémon* — **Normal**

> Additional software was installed to make it a better Pokémon. It began acting oddly, however.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 34 kg (75.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Adaptability**: Powers up same-type moves.
- **Download**: Adjusts power to the foe.
- **Analytic** *(hidden ability)*: Ups power when moving last.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
