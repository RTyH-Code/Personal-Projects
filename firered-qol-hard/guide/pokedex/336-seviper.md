# #336 Seviper

*The Fang Snake Pokémon* — **Poison**

> It sharpens its swordlike tail on hard rocks. It hides in tall grass and strikes unwary prey with venomous fangs.

Height 2.7 m (8′10″) · Weight 52.5 kg (115.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Shed Skin**: Heals the body by shedding.
- **Infiltrator** *(hidden ability)*: Passes through barriers.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 17**: Grass / cave Lv 33 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Seviper at the Four Island Day Care
