# #053 Persian

*The Classy Cat Pokémon* — **Normal**

> Has a vicious temperament. Beware if it raises its tail straight up. It is a signal that it is about to pounce and bite.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 32 kg (70.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Limber**: Prevents paralysis.
- **Technician**: Powers up weak moves.
- **Unnerve** *(hidden ability)*: Foes can't eat Berries.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
