# #083 Farfetch'd

*The Wild Duck Pokémon* — **Normal / Flying**

> It always walks about with a plant stalk clamped in its beak. The stalk is used for building its nest.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 15 kg (33.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Keen Eye**: Prevents loss of accuracy.
- **Inner Focus**: Prevents flinching.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

## Evolution

Farfetch'd does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Vermilion City House**: in-game trade for Spearow ("CH'DING")
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Farfetch'd at the Four Island Day Care
