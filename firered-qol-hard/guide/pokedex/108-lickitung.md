# #108 Lickitung

*The Licking Pokémon* — **Normal**

> Its tongue is twice the length of its body. It can be moved like an arm for grabbing food and attacking.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 65.5 kg (144.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Own Tempo**: Prevents confusion.
- **Oblivious**: Prevents attraction.
- **Cloud Nine** *(hidden ability)*: Negates weather effects.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 18 East Entrance 2F**: in-game trade for Golduck ("MARC")
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Lickitung at the Four Island Day Care
