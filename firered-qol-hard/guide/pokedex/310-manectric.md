# #310 Manectric

*The Discharge Pokémon* — **Electric**

> It rarely appears before people. It is said to nest where lightning has fallen.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 40.2 kg (88.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Static**: Paralyzes on contact.
- **Lightning Rod**: Absorbs Electric moves.
- **Minus** *(hidden ability)*: Powers up with Plus/Minus.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
