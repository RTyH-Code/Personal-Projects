# #225 Delibird

*The Delivery Pokémon* — **Ice / Flying**

> It nests at the edge of sharp cliffs. It spends all day carrying food to its awaiting chicks.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 16 kg (35.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Vital Spirit**: Prevents sleep.
- **Hustle**: Trades accuracy for power.
- **Insomnia** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents sleep.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Delibird does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Four Island Icefall Cave 1F**: Grass / cave Lv 30 (5%)
- **Four Island Icefall Cave B1F**: Grass / cave Lv 30 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Delibird at the Four Island Day Care
