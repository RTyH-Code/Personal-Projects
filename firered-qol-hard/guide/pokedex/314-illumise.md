# #314 Illumise

*The Firefly Pokémon* — **Bug**

> It guides Volbeat to draw signs in night skies. There are scientists that study the patterns it creates.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 17.7 kg (39.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Oblivious**: Prevents attraction.
- **Tinted Lens**: Ups not very effective hits.
- **Prankster** *(hidden ability)*: Status moves go first.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
