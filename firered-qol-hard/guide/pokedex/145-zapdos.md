# #145 Zapdos

*The Electric Pokémon* — **Electric / Flying**

> One of the legendary bird Pokémon. While it is flying, it makes crackling and snapping sounds.

Height 1.6 m (5′03″) · Weight 52.6 kg (116.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Pressure**: Raises foe's PP usage.
- **Static** *(hidden ability)*: Paralyzes on contact.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
