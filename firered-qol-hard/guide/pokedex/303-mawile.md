# #303 Mawile

*The Deceiver Pokémon* — **Steel / Fairy** *(originally Steel)*

> It uses its docile-looking face to lull foes into complacency, then bites with its huge, relentless jaws.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 11.5 kg (25.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hyper Cutter**: Prevents Attack reduction.
- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Sheer Force** *(hidden ability)*: Drops added effects for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

Mawile does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Rock Tunnel B1F**: Grass / cave Lv 15 (4%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Mawile at the Four Island Day Care
