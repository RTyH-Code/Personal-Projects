# #024 Arbok

*The Cobra Pokémon* — **Poison**

> The pattern on its belly appears to be a frightening face. Weak foes will flee just at the sight of the pattern.

Height 3.5 m (11′06″) · Weight 65 kg (143.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Shed Skin**: Heals the body by shedding.
- **Unnerve** *(hidden ability)*: Foes can't eat Berries.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
