# #072 Tentacool

*The Jellyfish Pokémon* — **Water / Poison**

> Its eyes are as transparent as crystals. From them, it shoots mysterious beams of light.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 45.5 kg (100.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Clear Body**: Prevents ability reduction.
- **Liquid Ooze**: Draining causes injury.
- **Rain Dish** *(hidden ability)*: Slight HP recovery in rain.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
