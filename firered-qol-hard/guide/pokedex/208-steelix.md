# #208 Steelix

*The Iron Snake Pokémon* — **Steel / Ground**

> It is said that if an Onix lives for over 100 years, its composition changes to become diamond-like.

Height 9.2 m (30′02″) · Weight 400 kg (881.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.
- **Sturdy**: Endures a KO at full HP.
- **Sheer Force** *(hidden ability)*: Drops added effects for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
