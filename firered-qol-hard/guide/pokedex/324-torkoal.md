# #324 Torkoal

*The Coal Pokémon* — **Fire**

> It burns coal inside its shell. If it is attacked, it belches thick, black smoke and flees.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 80.4 kg (177.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **White Smoke**: Prevents ability reduction.

- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Torkoal at the Four Island Day Care
