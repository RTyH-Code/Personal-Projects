# #357 Tropius

*The Fruit Pokémon* — **Grass / Flying**

> It lives in tropical jungles. The bunch of fruit around its neck is delicious. The fruit grows twice a year.

Height 2 m (6′07″) · Weight 100 kg (220.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Chlorophyll**: Raises Speed in sunshine.
- **Solar Power**: Ups Sp. Atk in sun, hurts.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

- **Five Island Meadow**: Grass / cave Lv 41 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Tropius at the Four Island Day Care
