# #332 Cacturne

*The Scarecrow Pokémon* — **Grass / Dark**

> It lives in deserts. It becomes active at night when it hunts for prey exhausted from the desert's heat.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 77.4 kg (170.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Sand Veil**: Ups evasion in a sandstorm.
- **Water Absorb** *(hidden ability)*: Changes water into HP.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
