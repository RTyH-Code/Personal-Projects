# #359 Absol

*The Disaster Pokémon* — **Dark**

> It appears when it senses an impending natural disaster. As a result, it was mistaken as a doom-bringer.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 47 kg (103.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Pressure**: Raises foe's PP usage.
- **Super Luck**: Ups critical-hit ratio.
- **Justified** *(hidden ability)*: Dark hits raise Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Absol does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Seven Island Sevault Canyon Entrance**: Grass / cave Lv 15 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Absol at the Four Island Day Care
