# #472 Gliscor

*The Fang Scorp Pokémon* — **Ground / Flying**

> It observes prey while hanging upside down from branches. When the chance presents itself, it swoops!

Height 2 m (6′07″) · Weight 42.5 kg (93.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hyper Cutter**: Prevents Attack reduction.
- **Sand Veil**: Ups evasion in a sandstorm.
- **Poison Heal** *(hidden ability)*: Poison restores HP.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
