# #300 Skitty

*The Kitten Pokémon* — **Normal**

> It is said to be difficult to earn its trust. However, it is extremely popular for its cute looks and behavior.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 11 kg (24.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Cute Charm**: Infatuates on contact.
- **Normalize**: All moves become Normal.
- **Wonder Skin** *(hidden ability)*: Evades status moves.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
