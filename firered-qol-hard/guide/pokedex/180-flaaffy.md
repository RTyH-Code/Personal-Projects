# #180 Flaaffy

*The Wool Pokémon* — **Electric**

> Its fluffy fleece easily stores electricity. Its rubbery hide keeps it from being electrocuted.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 13.3 kg (29.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Static**: Paralyzes on contact.
- **Plus** *(hidden ability)*: Powers up with Plus/Minus.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
