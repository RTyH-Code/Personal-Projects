# #154 Meganium

*The Herb Pokémon* — **Grass**

> Meganium's breath has the power to revive dead grass and plants. It can make them healthy again.

Height 1.8 m (5′11″) · Weight 100.5 kg (221.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Overgrow**: Ups Grass moves in a pinch.
- **Leaf Guard** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents status in sunlight.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
