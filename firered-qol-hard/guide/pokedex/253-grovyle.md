# #253 Grovyle

*The Wood Gecko Pokémon* — **Grass**

> Its strongly developed thigh muscles give it astounding agility and jumping performance.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 21.6 kg (47.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Overgrow**: Ups Grass moves in a pinch.
- **Unburden** *(hidden ability)*: Using an item ups Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
