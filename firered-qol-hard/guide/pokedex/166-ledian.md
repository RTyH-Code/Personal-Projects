# #166 Ledian

*The Five Star Pokémon* — **Bug / Flying**

> The star patterns on its back grow larger or smaller depending on the number of stars in the night sky.

Height 1.4 m (4′07″) · Weight 35.6 kg (78.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swarm**: Ups Bug moves in a pinch.
- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
