# #254 Sceptile

*The Forest Pokémon* — **Grass**

> The leaves on its forelegs are as sharp as swords. It agilely leaps about the branches of trees to strike.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 52.2 kg (115.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Overgrow**: Ups Grass moves in a pinch.
- **Unburden** *(hidden ability)*: Using an item ups Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
