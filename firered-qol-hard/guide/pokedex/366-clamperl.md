# #366 Clamperl

*The Bivalve Pokémon* — **Water**

> It is protected by a sturdy shell. Once in a lifetime, it makes a magnificent pearl.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 52.5 kg (115.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Shell Armor**: Blocks critical hits.
- **Rattled** *(hidden ability)*: Bug, Ghost, Dark hits up Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 20**: Super Rod Lv 15–25 (40%)
- **Six Island Outcast Island**: Super Rod Lv 15–25 (40%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Clamperl or Huntail or Gorebyss at the Four Island Day Care
