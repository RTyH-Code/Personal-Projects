# #193 Yanma

*The Clear Wing Pokémon* — **Bug / Flying**

> Its large eyes can scan 360 degrees. It looks in all directions to seek out insects as its prey.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 38 kg (83.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Speed Boost**: Gradually boosts Speed.
- **Compound Eyes**: Raises accuracy.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

- **Six Island Ruin Valley**: Grass / cave Lv 18 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Yanma at the Four Island Day Care
