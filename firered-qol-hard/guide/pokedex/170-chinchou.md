# #170 Chinchou

*The Angler Pokémon* — **Water / Electric**

> On the dark ocean floor, its only means of communication is its constantly flashing lights.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 12 kg (26.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Volt Absorb**: Turns electricity into HP.
- **Illuminate**: Encounter rate increases.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
