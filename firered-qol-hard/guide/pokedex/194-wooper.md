# #194 Wooper

*The Water Fish Pokémon* — **Water / Ground**

> When it walks around on the ground, it coats its body with a slimy, poisonous film.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 8.5 kg (18.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Damp**: Prevents self-destruction.
- **Water Absorb**: Changes water into HP.
- **Unaware** *(hidden ability)*: Ignores stat changes.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

- **Four Island**: Surfing Lv 5–25 (70%)
- **Four Island Icefall Cave Entrance**: Surfing Lv 5–15 (5%)

- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Wooper or Quagsire at the Four Island Day Care
