# #117 Seadra

*The Dragon Pokémon* — **Water**

> Its body bristles with sharp spikes. Carelessly trying to touch it could cause fainting from the spikes.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 25 kg (55.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Poison Point**: Poisons foe on contact.
- **Sniper**: Powers up critical hits.
- **Damp** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents self-destruction.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
