# #019 Rattata

*The Mouse Pokémon* — **Normal**

> Its fangs are long and very sharp. They grow continuously, so it gnaws on hard things to whittle them down.

Height 0.3 m (1′00″) · Weight 3.5 kg (7.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.
- **Guts**: Ups Attack if suffering.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
