# #102 Exeggcute

*The Egg Pokémon* — **Grass / Psychic**

> Even though it appears to be eggs of some sort, it was discovered to be a life-form more like plant seeds.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 2.5 kg (5.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Chlorophyll**: Raises Speed in sunshine.
- **Harvest** *(hidden ability)*: May restore a used Berry.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

## Where to find it

- **Safari Zone Center**: Grass / cave Lv 25 (10%)
- **Safari Zone East**: Grass / cave Lv 25 (10%)
- **Safari Zone North**: Grass / cave Lv 27 (10%)
- **Safari Zone West**: Grass / cave Lv 27 (10%)
- **Three Island Berry Forest**: Grass / cave Lv 35 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Exeggcute or Exeggutor at the Four Island Day Care
