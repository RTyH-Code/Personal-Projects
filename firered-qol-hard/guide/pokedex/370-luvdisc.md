# #370 Luvdisc

*The Rendezvous Pokémon* — **Water**

> During the spawning season, countless Luvdisc congregate at coral reefs, turning the waters pink.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 8.7 kg (19.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Hydration** *(hidden ability)*: Heals status in rain.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
