# #071 Victreebel

*The Flycatcher Pokémon* — **Grass / Poison**

> Lures prey into its mouth with a honeylike aroma. The helpless prey is melted with a dissolving fluid.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 15.5 kg (34.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Chlorophyll**: Raises Speed in sunshine.
- **Gluttony** *(hidden ability)*: Eats pinch Berries early.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
