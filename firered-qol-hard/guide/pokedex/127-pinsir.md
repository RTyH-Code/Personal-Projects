# #127 Pinsir

*The Stag Beetle Pokémon* — **Bug**

> Its two long pincer horns are powerful. Once they grip an enemy, they won't release until the foe is torn.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 55 kg (121.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hyper Cutter**: Prevents Attack reduction.
- **Mold Breaker**: Ignores the foe's ability.
- **Moxie** *(hidden ability)*: KOs raise Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Safari Zone Center**: Grass / cave Lv 28 (10%)
- **Safari Zone East**: Grass / cave Lv 26 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Pinsir at the Four Island Day Care
