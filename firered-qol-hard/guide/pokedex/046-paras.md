# #046 Paras

*The Mushroom Pokémon* — **Bug / Grass**

> Growing out of the bug's back are mushrooms called tochukaso. The mushrooms grow with the bug host.

Height 0.3 m (1′00″) · Weight 5.4 kg (11.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Effect Spore**: Leaves spores on contact.
- **Dry Skin**: Water heals, fire and sun hurt.
- **Damp** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents self-destruction.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
