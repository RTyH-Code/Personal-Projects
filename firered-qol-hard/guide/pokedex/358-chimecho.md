# #358 Chimecho

*The Wind Chime Pokémon* — **Psychic**

> It travels by riding on winds. It cleverly uses its long tail to pluck nuts and berries, which it loves to eat.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 1 kg (2.2 lbs)

- **Pokémon Tower 7F**: Grass / cave Lv 19 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Chimecho at the Four Island Day Care
