# #356 Dusclops

*The Beckon Pokémon* — **Ghost**

> Its body is entirely hollow. When it opens its mouth, it sucks everything in as if it were a black hole.

Height 1.6 m (5′03″) · Weight 30.6 kg (67.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Pressure**: Raises foe's PP usage.
- **Frisk** *(hidden ability)*: Checks the foe's item.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
