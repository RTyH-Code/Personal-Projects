# #291 Ninjask

*The Ninja Pokémon* — **Bug / Flying**

> This Pokémon is so quick, it is said to be able to avoid any attack. It loves to feed on tree sap.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 12 kg (26.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Speed Boost**: Gradually boosts Speed.
- **Infiltrator** *(hidden ability)*: Passes through barriers.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
