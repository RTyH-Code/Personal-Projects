# #215 Sneasel

*The Sharp Claw Pokémon* — **Dark / Ice**

> Vicious in nature, it drives Pidgey from their nests and feasts on the eggs that are left behind.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 28 kg (61.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Inner Focus**: Prevents flinching.
- **Keen Eye**: Prevents loss of accuracy.
- **Pickpocket** *(hidden ability)*: Steals items on contact.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Sneasel does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Four Island Icefall Cave 1F**: Grass / cave Lv 31 (5%)
- **Four Island Icefall Cave B1F**: Grass / cave Lv 23 (4%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Sneasel at the Four Island Day Care
