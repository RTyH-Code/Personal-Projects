# #900 Kleavor

*The Axe Pokémon* — **Bug / Rock**

> Its axes are made of stone. It hews large trees with them and is said to be a wild, aggressive Pokémon.

Height 1.8 m (5′11″) · Weight 89 kg (196.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swarm**: Ups Bug moves in a pinch.
- **Sheer Force**: Drops added effects for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
