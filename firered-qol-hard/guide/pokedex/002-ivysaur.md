# #002 Ivysaur

*The Seed Pokémon* — **Grass / Poison**

> There is a plant bulb on its back. When it absorbs nutrients, the bulb is said to blossom into a large flower.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 13 kg (28.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Overgrow**: Ups Grass moves in a pinch.
- **Chlorophyll** *(hidden ability)*: Raises Speed in sunshine.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
