# #367 Huntail

*The Deep Sea Pokémon* — **Water**

> It lives deep in the sea where no light ever filters down. It lights up its small fishlike tail to attract prey.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 27 kg (59.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
