# #217 Ursaring

*The Hibernator Pokémon* — **Normal**

> With its ability to distinguish any smell, it unfailingly finds all food buried deep underground.

Height 1.8 m (5′11″) · Weight 125.8 kg (277.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Guts**: Ups Attack if suffering.
- **Quick Feet**: Ups Speed if suffering.
- **Unnerve** *(hidden ability)*: Foes can't eat Berries.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
