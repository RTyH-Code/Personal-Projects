# #081 Magnemite

*The Magnet Pokémon* — **Electric / Steel**

> It moves while constantly hovering. It discharges Thunder Wave and so on from the units at its sides.

Height 0.3 m (1′00″) · Weight 6 kg (13.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Magnet Pull**: Traps Steel-type Pokémon.
- **Sturdy**: Negates 1-hit KO attacks.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
