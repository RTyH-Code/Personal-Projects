# #272 Ludicolo

*The Carefree Pokémon* — **Water / Grass**

> The rhythm of bright, festive music activates Ludicolo's cells, making it more powerful.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 55 kg (121.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Rain Dish**: Slight HP recovery in rain.
- **Own Tempo** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents confusion.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
