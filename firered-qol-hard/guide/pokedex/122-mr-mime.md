# #122 Mr. Mime

*The Barrier Pokémon* — **Psychic**

> It is adept at conning people. It is said to be able to create walls out of thin air by miming.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 54.5 kg (120.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Soundproof**: Avoids sound-based moves.

## Evolution

Mr. Mime does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Route 2 House**: in-game trade for Abra ("MIMIEN")
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Mr. Mime at the Four Island Day Care
