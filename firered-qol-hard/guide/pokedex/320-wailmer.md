# #320 Wailmer

*The Ball Whale Pokémon* — **Water**

> When it sucks in a large volume of seawater, it becomes like a big, bouncy ball. It eats a ton of food daily.

Height 2 m (6′07″) · Weight 130 kg (286.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Water Veil**: Prevents burns.
- **Oblivious**: Prevents attraction.
- **Pressure** *(hidden ability)*: Raises foe's PP usage.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
