# #296 Makuhita

*The Guts Pokémon* — **Fighting**

> It grows stronger by enduring harsh training. It is a gutsy Pokémon that can withstand any attack.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 86.4 kg (190.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Thick Fat**: Heat-and-cold protection.
- **Guts**: Ups Attack if suffering.
- **Sheer Force** *(hidden ability)*: Drops added effects for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
