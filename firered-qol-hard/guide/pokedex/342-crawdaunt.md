# #342 Crawdaunt

*The Rogue Pokémon* — **Water / Dark**

> A rough customer that wildly flails its giant claws. It is said to be extremely hard to raise.

Height 1.1 m (3′07″) · Weight 32.8 kg (72.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hyper Cutter**: Prevents Attack reduction.
- **Shell Armor**: Blocks critical hits.
- **Adaptability** *(hidden ability)*: Powers up same-type moves.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
