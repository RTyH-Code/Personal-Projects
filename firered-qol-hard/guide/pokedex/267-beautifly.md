# #267 Beautifly

*The Butterfly Pokémon* — **Bug / Flying**

> Despite its appearance, it has an aggressive nature. It attacks by jabbing with its long, thin mouth.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 28.4 kg (62.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swarm**: Ups Bug moves in a pinch.
- **Rivalry** *(hidden ability)*: Strong vs. the same gender.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
