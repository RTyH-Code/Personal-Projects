# #382 Kyogre

*The Sea Basin Pokémon* — **Water**

> This Pokémon is said to have expanded the sea by bringing heavy rains. It has the power to control water.

Height 4.5 m (14′09″) · Weight 352 kg (776.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Drizzle**: Summons rain in battle.
