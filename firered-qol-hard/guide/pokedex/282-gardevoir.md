# #282 Gardevoir

*The Embrace Pokémon* — **Psychic / Fairy** *(originally Psychic)*

> It has the power to predict the future. Its power peaks when it is protecting its Trainer.

Height 1.6 m (5′03″) · Weight 48.4 kg (106.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Synchronize**: Passes on status problems.
- **Trace**: Copies special ability.
- **Telepathy** *(hidden ability)*: Dodges allies' attacks.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
