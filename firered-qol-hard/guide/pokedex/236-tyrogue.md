# #236 Tyrogue

*The Scuffle Pokémon* — **Fighting**

> Even though it is small, it can't be ignored because it will slug any handy target without warning.

Height 0.7 m (2′04″) · Weight 21 kg (46.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Guts**: Ups Attack if suffering.
- **Steadfast**: Flinching raises Speed.
- **Vital Spirit** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents sleep.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
