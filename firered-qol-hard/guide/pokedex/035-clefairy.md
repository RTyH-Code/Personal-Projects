# #035 Clefairy

*The Fairy Pokémon* — **Normal**

> Its adorable appearance makes it popular as a pet. However, it is rare and difficult to find.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 7.5 kg (16.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Cute Charm**: Infatuates on contact.

- [Cleffa](173-cleffa.md) → **Clefairy**: Level up with high friendship (220+)
  - **Clefairy** → [Clefable](036-clefable.md): Use a Moon Stone

## Where to find it

- **Mt. Moon 1F**: Grass / cave Lv 8 (1%)
- **Mt. Moon B2F**: Grass / cave Lv 10–12 (6%)
- **Celadon City Game Corner Prize Room**: Game Corner prize (500 coins), Lv 8
- Evolve [Cleffa](173-cleffa.md)
