# #169 Crobat

*The Bat Pokémon* — **Poison / Flying**

> The development of wings on its legs enables it to fly fast but also makes it tough to stop and rest.

Height 1.8 m (5′11″) · Weight 75 kg (165.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Inner Focus**: Prevents flinching.
- **Infiltrator** *(hidden ability)*: Passes through barriers.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
