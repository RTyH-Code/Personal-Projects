# #172 Pichu

*The Tiny Mouse Pokémon* — **Electric**

> Despite its small size, it can zap even adult humans. However, if it does so, it also surprises itself.

- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a [Pikachu](025-pikachu.md) at the Four Island Day Care
