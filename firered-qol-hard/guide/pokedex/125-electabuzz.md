# #125 Electabuzz

*The Electric Pokémon* — **Electric**

> It loves to feed on strong electricity. It occasionally appears around large power plants and so on.

Height 1.1 m (3′07″) · Weight 30 kg (66.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Static**: Paralyzes on contact.
- **Vital Spirit** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents sleep.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
