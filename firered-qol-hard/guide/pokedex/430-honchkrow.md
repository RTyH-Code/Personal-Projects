# #430 Honchkrow

*The Big Boss Pokémon* — **Dark / Flying**

> Becoming active at night, it is known to swarm with numerous Murkrow in tow. It never forgives a mistake.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 27.3 kg (60.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Insomnia**: Prevents sleep.
- **Super Luck**: Ups critical-hit ratio.
- **Moxie** *(hidden ability)*: KOs raise Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
