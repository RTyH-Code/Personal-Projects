# #133 Eevee

*The Evolution Pokémon* — **Normal**

> An extremely rare Pokémon that may evolve in a number of different ways depending on stimuli.

Height 0.3 m (1′00″) · Weight 6.5 kg (14.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.
- **Adaptability**: Powers up same-type moves.
- **Anticipation** *(hidden ability)*: Senses dangerous moves.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

- **Eevee** → [Jolteon](135-jolteon.md): Use a Thunder Stone
- **Eevee** → [Vaporeon](134-vaporeon.md): Use a Water Stone
- **Eevee** → [Flareon](136-flareon.md): Use a Fire Stone
- **Eevee** → [Espeon](196-espeon.md): Use a Sun Stone *(originally: Level up with high friendship during the day)*
- **Eevee** → [Umbreon](197-umbreon.md): Use a Moon Stone *(originally: Level up with high friendship at night)*

## Where to find it

- **Celadon City Condominiums Roof Room**: Gift, Lv 25
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Eevee or Jolteon or Vaporeon or Flareon or Espeon or Umbreon at the Four Island Day Care
