# #065 Alakazam

*The Psi Pokémon* — **Psychic**

> It does not like physical attacks very much. Instead, it freely uses extra-sensory powers to defeat foes.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 48 kg (105.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Synchronize**: Passes on status problems.
- **Inner Focus**: Prevents flinching.
- **Magic Guard** *(hidden ability)*: Only hurt by attacks.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
