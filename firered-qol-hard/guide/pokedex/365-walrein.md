# #365 Walrein

*The Ice Break Pokémon* — **Ice / Water**

> It swims through icy seas while shattering ice floes with its large tusks. It is protected by its thick blubber.

Height 1.4 m (4′07″) · Weight 150.6 kg (332.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Thick Fat**: Heat-and-cold protection.
- **Ice Body**: Slight HP recovery in hail.
- **Oblivious** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents attraction.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
