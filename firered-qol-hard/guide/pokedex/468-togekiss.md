# #468 Togekiss

*The Jubilee Pokémon* — **Fairy / Flying**

> It shares many blessings with people who respect one another's rights and avoid needless strife.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 38 kg (83.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hustle**: Trades accuracy for power.
- **Serene Grace**: Promotes added effects.
- **Super Luck** *(hidden ability)*: Ups critical-hit ratio.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
