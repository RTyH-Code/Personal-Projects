# #206 Dunsparce

*The Land Snake Pokémon* — **Normal**

> If spotted, it escapes by burrowing with its tail. It can hover just slightly using its wings.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 14 kg (30.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Serene Grace**: Promotes added effects.
- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

## Evolution

Dunsparce does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Three Island Port**: Grass / cave Lv 5–35 (100%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Dunsparce at the Four Island Day Care
