# #064 Kadabra

*The Psi Pokémon* — **Psychic**

> It happened one morning - a boy with extrasensory powers awoke in bed transformed into Kadabra.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 56.5 kg (124.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Synchronize**: Passes on status problems.
- **Inner Focus**: Prevents flinching.
- **Magic Guard** *(hidden ability)*: Only hurt by attacks.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
