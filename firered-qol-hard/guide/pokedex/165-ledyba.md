# #165 Ledyba

*The Five Star Pokémon* — **Bug / Flying**

> When the weather turns cold, numerous Ledyba gather from everywhere to cluster and keep each other warm.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 10.8 kg (23.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swarm**: Ups Bug moves in a pinch.
- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

- **Six Island Pattern Bush**: Grass / cave Lv 9–14 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Ledyba or Ledian at the Four Island Day Care
