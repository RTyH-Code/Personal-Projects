# #146 Moltres

*The Flame Pokémon* — **Fire / Flying**

> One of the legendary bird Pokémon. Those seeing it are overwhelmed by its orange wings that seem to be on fire.

Height 2 m (6′07″) · Weight 60 kg (132.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Pressure**: Raises foe's PP usage.
- **Flame Body** *(hidden ability)*: Burns the foe on contact.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
