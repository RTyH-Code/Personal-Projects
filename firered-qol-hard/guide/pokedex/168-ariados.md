# #168 Ariados

*The Long Leg Pokémon* — **Bug / Poison**

> A single strand of a special string is endlessly spun out of its rear. The string leads back to its nest.

Height 1.1 m (3′07″) · Weight 33.5 kg (73.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swarm**: Ups Bug moves in a pinch.
- **Insomnia**: Prevents sleep.
- **Sniper** *(hidden ability)*: Powers up critical hits.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
