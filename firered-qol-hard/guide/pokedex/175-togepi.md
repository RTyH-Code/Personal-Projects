# #175 Togepi

*The Spike Ball Pokémon* — **Normal**

> A proverb claims that happiness will come to anyone who can make a sleeping Togepi stand up.

Height 0.3 m (1′00″) · Weight 1.5 kg (3.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hustle**: Trades accuracy for power.
- **Serene Grace**: Promotes added effects.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
