# #323 Camerupt

*The Eruption Pokémon* — **Fire / Ground**

> If angered, the humps on its back erupt in a shower of molten lava. It lives in the craters of volcanoes.

Height 1.9 m (6′03″) · Weight 220 kg (485.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Magma Armor**: Prevents freezing.
- **Solid Rock**: Weakens super effective hits.
- **Anger Point** *(hidden ability)*: Critical hits max Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
