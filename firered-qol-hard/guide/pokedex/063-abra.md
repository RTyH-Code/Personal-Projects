# #063 Abra

*The Psi Pokémon* — **Psychic**

> It sleeps for 18 hours a day. It uses a variety of extrasensory powers even while asleep.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 19.5 kg (43.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Synchronize**: Passes on status problems.
- **Inner Focus**: Prevents flinching.
- **Magic Guard** *(hidden ability)*: Only hurt by attacks.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
