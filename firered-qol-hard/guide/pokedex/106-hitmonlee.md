# #106 Hitmonlee

*The Kicking Pokémon* — **Fighting**

> The legs freely contract and stretch. The stretchy legs allow it to hit a distant foe with a rising kick.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 49.8 kg (109.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Limber**: Prevents paralysis.
- **Reckless**: Powers up recoil moves.
- **Unburden** *(hidden ability)*: Using an item ups Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
