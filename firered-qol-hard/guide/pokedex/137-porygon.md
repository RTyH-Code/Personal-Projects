# #137 Porygon

*The Virtual Pokémon* — **Normal**

> Using the most advanced technologies, scientists finally succeeded in making the first artificial Pokémon.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 36.5 kg (80.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Trace**: Copies special ability.
- **Analytic** *(hidden ability)*: Ups power when moving last.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

- **Porygon** → [Porygon2](233-porygon2.md): Use an Up-Grade *(originally: Trade holding Up-Grade)*

## Where to find it

- **Celadon City Game Corner Prize Room**: Game Corner prize (9999 coins), Lv 26
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Porygon or Porygon2 at the Four Island Day Care
