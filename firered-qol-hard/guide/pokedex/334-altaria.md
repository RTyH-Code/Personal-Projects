# #334 Altaria

*The Humming Pokémon* — **Dragon / Flying**

> If you hear a beautiful melody trilling deep among mountains far from people, it is Altaria's humming.

Height 1.1 m (3′07″) · Weight 20.6 kg (45.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Natural Cure**: Heals upon switching out.
- **Cloud Nine** *(hidden ability)*: Negates weather effects.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
