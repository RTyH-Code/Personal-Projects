# #198 Murkrow

*The Darkness Pokémon* — **Dark / Flying**

> It is said that when chased, it lures its attacker onto dark mountain trails where the foe will get lost.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 2.1 kg (4.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Insomnia**: Prevents sleep.
- **Super Luck**: Ups critical-hit ratio.
- **Prankster** *(hidden ability)*: Status moves go first.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
