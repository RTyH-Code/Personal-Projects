# #424 Ambipom

*The Long Tail Pokémon* — **Normal**

> Its split tails are so adept at handling things that Ambipom now rarely uses its hands.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 20.3 kg (44.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Technician**: Powers up weak moves.
- **Pickup**: May pick up items.
- **Skill Link** *(hidden ability)*: Multi-hit moves hit 5 times.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
