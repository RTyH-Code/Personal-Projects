# #048 Venonat

*The Insect Pokémon* — **Bug / Poison**

> Its eyes act as radar, enabling it to be active in darkness. The eyes can also shoot powerful beams.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 30 kg (66.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Compound Eyes**: Raises accuracy.
- **Tinted Lens**: Ups not very effective hits.
- **Run Away** *(hidden ability)*: Makes escaping easier.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
