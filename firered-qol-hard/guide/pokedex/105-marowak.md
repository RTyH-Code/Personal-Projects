# #105 Marowak

*The Bone Keeper Pokémon* — **Ground**

> It is small and was originally very weak. Its temperament turned ferocious when it began using bones.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 45 kg (99.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.
- **Lightning Rod**: Draws electrical moves.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
