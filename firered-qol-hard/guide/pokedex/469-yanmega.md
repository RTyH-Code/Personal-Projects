# #469 Yanmega

*The Ogre Darner Pokémon* — **Bug / Flying**

> This six-legged Pokémon is easily capable of carrying an adult in flight. Its wings beat at high speed.

Height 1.9 m (6′03″) · Weight 51.5 kg (113.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Speed Boost**: Gradually boosts Speed.
- **Tinted Lens**: Ups not very effective hits.
- **Frisk** *(hidden ability)*: Checks the foe's item.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
