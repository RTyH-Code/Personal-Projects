# #114 Tangela

*The Vine Pokémon* — **Grass**

> Blue plant vines cloak the Pokémon's identity in a tangled mass. It entangles anything that gets close.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 35 kg (77.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Chlorophyll**: Raises Speed in sunshine.
- **Leaf Guard**: Prevents status in sunlight.
- **Regenerator** *(hidden ability)*: Heals when switched out.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 21 North**: Grass / cave Lv 17–28 (100%)
- **Route 21 South**: Grass / cave Lv 17–28 (100%)
- **One Island Treasure Beach**: Grass / cave Lv 33–35 (30%)
- **Cinnabar Island Pokémon Lab Lounge**: in-game trade for Venonat ("TANGENY")
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Tangela or Tangrowth at the Four Island Day Care
