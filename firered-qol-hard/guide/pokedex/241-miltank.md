# #241 Miltank

*The Milk Cow Pokémon* — **Normal**

> If it has just had a baby, the milk it produces contains much more nutrition than usual.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 75.5 kg (166.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Thick Fat**: Heat-and-cold protection.
- **Scrappy**: Hits Ghost-type Pokémon.
- **Sap Sipper** *(hidden ability)*: Grass moves raise Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

Miltank does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Five Island Meadow**: Grass / cave Lv 15 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Miltank at the Four Island Day Care
