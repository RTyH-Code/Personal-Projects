# #144 Articuno

*The Freeze Pokémon* — **Ice / Flying**

> One of the legendary bird Pokémon. With its long tail trailing behind, its flying form is magnificent.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 55.4 kg (122.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Pressure**: Raises foe's PP usage.
- **Snow Cloak** *(hidden ability)*: Ups evasion in hail.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
