# #123 Scyther

*The Mantis Pokémon* — **Bug / Flying**

> It tears and shreds prey with its wickedly sharp scythes. It very rarely spreads its wings to fly.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 56 kg (123.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swarm**: Ups Bug moves in a pinch.
- **Technician**: Powers up weak moves.
- **Steadfast** *(hidden ability)*: Flinching raises Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Safari Zone Center**: Grass / cave Lv 23 (4%)
- **Safari Zone East**: Grass / cave Lv 28 (1%)
- **Celadon City Game Corner Prize Room**: Game Corner prize (5500 coins), Lv 25
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Scyther or Scizor at the Four Island Day Care
