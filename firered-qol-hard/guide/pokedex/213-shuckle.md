# #213 Shuckle

*The Mold Pokémon* — **Bug / Rock**

> It stores Berries inside its shell. To avoid attacks, it hides beneath rocks and remains completely still.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 20.5 kg (45.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Sturdy**: Endures a KO at full HP.
- **Gluttony**: Eats pinch Berries early.
- **Contrary** *(hidden ability)*: Inverts stat changes.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Shuckle does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Six Island Altering Cave**: Grass / cave Lv 20–24 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Shuckle at the Four Island Day Care
