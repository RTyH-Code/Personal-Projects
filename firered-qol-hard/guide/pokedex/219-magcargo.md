# #219 Magcargo

*The Lava Pokémon* — **Fire / Rock**

> Its brittle shell occasionally spouts intense flames that circulate throughout its body.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 55 kg (121.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Magma Armor**: Prevents freezing.
- **Flame Body**: Burns the foe on contact.
- **Weak Armor** *(hidden ability)*: Hits lower Def, raise Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
