# #171 Lanturn

*The Light Pokémon* — **Water / Electric**

> It blinds prey with an intense burst of light, then swallows the immobilized prey in a single gulp.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 22.5 kg (49.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Volt Absorb**: Turns electricity into HP.
- **Illuminate**: Encounter rate increases.
- **Water Absorb** *(hidden ability)*: Changes water into HP.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
