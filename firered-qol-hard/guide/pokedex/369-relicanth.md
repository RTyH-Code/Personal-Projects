# #369 Relicanth

*The Longevity Pokémon* — **Water / Rock**

> It has remained unchanged for 100 million years. It was discovered during a deep-sea exploration.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 23.4 kg (51.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.
- **Sturdy** *(hidden ability)*: Endures a KO at full HP.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Relicanth does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Seven Island Tanoby Ruins**: Super Rod Lv 15–25 (40%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Relicanth at the Four Island Day Care
