# #345 Lileep

*The Sea Lily Pokémon* — **Rock / Grass**

> It became extinct roughly 100 million years ago. It was regenerated from a fossil using advanced techniques.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 23.8 kg (52.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Suction Cups**: Firmly anchors the body.
- **Storm Drain** *(hidden ability)*: Water moves raise Sp. Atk.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Seven Island Sevault Canyon**: Rock Smash Lv 35–50 (5%)
- **Cinnabar Island Pokémon Lab Experiment Room**: Gift, Lv 5
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Lileep or Cradily at the Four Island Day Care
