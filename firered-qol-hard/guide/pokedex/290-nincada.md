# #290 Nincada

*The Trainee Pokémon* — **Bug / Ground**

> Because it lived almost entirely underground, it is nearly blind. It uses its antennae instead.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 5.5 kg (12.1 lbs)

## Evolution

- **Nincada** → [Ninjask](291-ninjask.md): Level 20
- **Nincada** → [Shedinja](292-shedinja.md): Level 20 as Nincada evolves, with an empty party slot and a spare Poké Ball

## Where to find it

- **Route 24**: Grass / cave Lv 8 (4%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Nincada or Ninjask or Shedinja at the Four Island Day Care
