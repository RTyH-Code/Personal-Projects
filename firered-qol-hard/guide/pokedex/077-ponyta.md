# #077 Ponyta

*The Fire Horse Pokémon* — **Fire**

> Its body is light, and its legs are incredibly powerful. It can clear Ayers Rock in one leap.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 30 kg (66.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.
- **Flash Fire**: Powers up if hit by fire.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

- **Mt. Ember Exterior**: Grass / cave Lv 30–33 (30%)
- **One Island Kindle Road**: Grass / cave Lv 34 (20%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Ponyta or Rapidash at the Four Island Day Care
