# #036 Clefable

*The Fairy Pokémon* — **Fairy** *(originally Normal)*

> It has an acute sense of hearing. It can easily hear a pin being dropped nearly 1,100 yards away.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 40 kg (88.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Cute Charm**: Infatuates on contact.
- **Magic Guard**: Only hurt by attacks.
- **Unaware** *(hidden ability)*: Ignores stat changes.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
