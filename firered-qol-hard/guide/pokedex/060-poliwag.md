# #060 Poliwag

*The Tadpole Pokémon* — **Water**

> Its slick black skin is thin and damp. A part of its internal organs can be seen through the skin as a spiral pattern.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 12.4 kg (27.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Water Absorb**: Changes water into HP.
- **Damp**: Prevents self-destruction.
- **Swift Swim** *(hidden ability)*: Raises Speed in rain.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
