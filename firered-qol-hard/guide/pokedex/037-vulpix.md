- **Route 7**: Grass / cave Lv 17 (4%)
- **Route 8**: Grass / cave Lv 17 (5%)
- **Pokémon Mansion 1F**: Grass / cave Lv 30 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Vulpix or Ninetales at the Four Island Day Care
