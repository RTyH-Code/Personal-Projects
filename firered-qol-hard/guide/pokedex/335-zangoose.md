# #335 Zangoose

*The Cat Ferret Pokémon* — **Normal**

> If it comes across a Seviper, its fur bristles and it assumes its battle pose. Its sharp claws are its best weapon.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 40.3 kg (88.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Immunity**: Prevents poisoning.
- **Toxic Boost** *(hidden ability)*: Ups Attack when poisoned.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

Zangoose does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Route 17**: Grass / cave Lv 32 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Zangoose at the Four Island Day Care
