# #222 Corsola

*The Coral Pokémon* — **Water / Rock**

> In a south sea nation, the people live in communities that are built on groups of these Pokémon.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 5 kg (11.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hustle**: Trades accuracy for power.
- **Natural Cure**: Heals upon switching out.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 20**: Surfing Lv 30–35 (4%)
- **One Island Treasure Beach**: Surfing Lv 35–40 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Corsola at the Four Island Day Care
