# #023 Ekans

*The Snake Pokémon* — **Poison**

> A very common sight in grassland, etc. It flicks its tongue in and out to sense danger in its surroundings.

Height 2 m (6′07″) · Weight 6.9 kg (15.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Shed Skin**: Heals the body by shedding.
- **Unnerve** *(hidden ability)*: Foes can't eat Berries.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
