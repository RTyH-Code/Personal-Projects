# #023 Ekans

*The Snake Pokémon* — **Poison**

> A very common sight in grassland, etc. It flicks its tongue in and out to sense danger in its surroundings.

Height 2 m (6′07″) · Weight 6.9 kg (15.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Shed Skin**: Heals the body by shedding.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
