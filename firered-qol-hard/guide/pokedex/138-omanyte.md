# #138 Omanyte

*The Spiral Pokémon* — **Rock / Water**

> A prehistoric Pokémon that lived in the primordial sea, it swims by twisting its 10 tentacles about.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 7.5 kg (16.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Shell Armor**: Blocks critical hits.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
