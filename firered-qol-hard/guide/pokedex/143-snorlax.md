# #143 Snorlax

*The Sleeping Pokémon* — **Normal**

> It is not satisfied unless it eats over 880 pounds of food every day. When it is done eating, it goes promptly to sleep.

Height 2.1 m (6′11″) · Weight 460 kg (1014.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Immunity**: Prevents poisoning.
- **Thick Fat**: Heat-and-cold protection.
- **Gluttony** *(hidden ability)*: Eats pinch Berries early.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Snorlax does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Route 12**: one-time encounter, Lv 30
- **Route 16**: one-time encounter, Lv 30
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Snorlax at the Four Island Day Care
