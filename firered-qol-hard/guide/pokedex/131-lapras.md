# #131 Lapras

*The Transport Pokémon* — **Water / Ice**

> Its high intelligence enables it to understand human speech. It likes to ferry people on its back.

Height 2.5 m (8′02″) · Weight 220 kg (485.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Water Absorb**: Changes water into HP.
- **Shell Armor**: Blocks critical hits.
- **Hydration** *(hidden ability)*: Heals status in rain.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Lapras does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Four Island Icefall Cave Back**: Surfing Lv 30–45 (1%)
- **Silph Co. 7F**: Gift, Lv 25
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Lapras at the Four Island Day Care
