# #227 Skarmory

*The Armor Bird Pokémon* — **Steel / Flying**

> After nesting in bramble bushes, the wings of its chicks grow hard from scratches by thorns.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 50.5 kg (111.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Keen Eye**: Prevents loss of accuracy.
- **Sturdy**: Endures a KO at full HP.
- **Weak Armor** *(hidden ability)*: Hits lower Def, raise Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Skarmory does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Seven Island Sevault Canyon**: Grass / cave Lv 30 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Skarmory at the Four Island Day Care
