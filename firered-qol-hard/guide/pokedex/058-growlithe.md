# #058 Growlithe

*The Puppy Pokémon* — **Fire**

> Very friendly and faithful to people. It will try to repel enemies by barking and biting.

Height 0.7 m (2′04″) · Weight 19 kg (41.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Flash Fire**: Powers up if hit by fire.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
