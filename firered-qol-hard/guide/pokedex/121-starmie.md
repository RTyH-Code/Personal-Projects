# #121 Starmie

*The Mysterious Pokémon* — **Water / Psychic**

> This Pokémon has a geometric body. Because of its body, the locals suspect that it is an alien creature.

Height 1.1 m (3′07″) · Weight 80 kg (176.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Illuminate**: Encounter rate increases.
- **Natural Cure**: Heals upon switching out.
- **Analytic** *(hidden ability)*: Ups power when moving last.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
