# #252 Treecko

*The Wood Gecko Pokémon* — **Grass**

> It quickly scales even vertical walls. It senses humidity with its tail to predict the next day's weather.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 5 kg (11.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Overgrow**: Ups Grass moves in a pinch.
- **Unburden** *(hidden ability)*: Using an item ups Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
