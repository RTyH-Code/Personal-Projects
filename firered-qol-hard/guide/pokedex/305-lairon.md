# #305 Lairon

*The Iron Armor Pokémon* — **Steel / Rock**

> It habitually shows off its strength with the size of sparks it creates by ramming its steel body into boulders.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 120 kg (264.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Sturdy**: Negates 1-hit KO attacks.
- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.
- **Heavy Metal** *(hidden ability)*: Doubles its weight.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
