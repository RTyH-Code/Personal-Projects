# #273 Seedot

*The Acorn Pokémon* — **Grass**

> If it remains still, it becomes impossible to distinguish from real nuts. It delights in surprising foraging Pidgey.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 4 kg (8.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Chlorophyll**: Raises Speed in sunshine.
- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

- **Seedot** → [Nuzleaf](274-nuzleaf.md): Level 14
  - [Nuzleaf](274-nuzleaf.md) → [Shiftry](275-shiftry.md): Use a Leaf Stone

## Where to find it

- **Viridian Forest**: Grass / cave Lv 3 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Seedot or Nuzleaf or Shiftry at the Four Island Day Care
