# #096 Drowzee

*The Hypnosis Pokémon* — **Psychic**

> A descendent of the legendary animal baku, which is said to eat dreams. It is skilled at hypnotism.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 32.4 kg (71.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Insomnia**: Prevents sleep.
- **Forewarn**: Senses the foe's best move.
- **Inner Focus** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents flinching.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
