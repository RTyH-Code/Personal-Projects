# #055 Golduck

*The Duck Pokémon* — **Water**

> The forelegs are webbed, helping to make it an adept swimmer. It can be seen swimming elegantly in lakes, etc.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 76.6 kg (168.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Damp**: Prevents self-destruction.
- **Cloud Nine**: Negates weather effects.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
