# #278 Wingull

*The Seagull Pokémon* — **Water / Flying**

> It rides upon ocean winds as if it were a glider. In the winter, it hides food around its nest.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 9.5 kg (20.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Keen Eye**: Prevents loss of accuracy.
- **Hydration**: Heals status in rain.
- **Rain Dish** *(hidden ability)*: Slight HP recovery in rain.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
