# #328 Trapinch

*The Ant Pit Pokémon* — **Ground**

> It lives in arid deserts. It makes a sloping pit trap in sand where it patiently awaits prey.

Height 0.7 m (2′04″) · Weight 15 kg (33.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hyper Cutter**: Prevents Attack reduction.
- **Arena Trap**: Prevents fleeing.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
