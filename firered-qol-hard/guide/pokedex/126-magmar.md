# #126 Magmar

*The Spitfire Pokémon* — **Fire**

> Found near the mouth of a volcano. This fire-breather's body temperature is nearly 2,200 degrees Fahrenheit.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 44.5 kg (98.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Flame Body**: Burns the foe on contact.
- **Vital Spirit** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents sleep.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
