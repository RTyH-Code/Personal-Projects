# #197 Umbreon

*The Moonlight Pokémon* — **Dark**

> When darkness falls, the rings on its body begin to glow, striking fear in the hearts of anyone nearby.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 27 kg (59.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Synchronize**: Passes on status problems.
- **Inner Focus** *(hidden ability)*: Prevents flinching.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
