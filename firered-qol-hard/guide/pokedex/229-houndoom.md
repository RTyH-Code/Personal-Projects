# #229 Houndoom

*The Dark Pokémon* — **Dark / Fire**

> Upon hearing its eerie howls, other Pokémon get the shivers and head straight back to their nests.

Height 1.4 m (4′07″) · Weight 35 kg (77.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.
- **Flash Fire**: Powers up if hit by fire.
- **Unnerve** *(hidden ability)*: Foes can't eat Berries.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
