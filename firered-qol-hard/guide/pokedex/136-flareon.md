# #136 Flareon

*The Flame Pokémon* — **Fire**

> It has a flame bag inside its body. After inhaling deeply, it blows out flames of nearly 3,100 degrees Fahrenheit.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 25 kg (55.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Flash Fire**: Powers up if hit by fire.
- **Guts** *(hidden ability)*: Ups Attack if suffering.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
