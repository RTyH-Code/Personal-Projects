# #034 Nidoking

*The Drill Pokémon* — **Poison / Ground**

> It is recognized by its rock-hard hide and its extended horn. Be careful with the horn as it contains venom.

Height 1.4 m (4′07″) · Weight 62 kg (136.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Poison Point**: Poisons foe on contact.
- **Rivalry**: Strong vs. the same gender.
- **Sheer Force** *(hidden ability)*: Drops added effects for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
