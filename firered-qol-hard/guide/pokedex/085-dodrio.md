# #085 Dodrio

*The Triple Bird Pokémon* — **Normal / Flying**

> An odd species that is rarely found. The three heads respectively represent joy, sadness, and anger.

Height 1.8 m (5′11″) · Weight 85.2 kg (187.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.
- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.
- **Tangled Feet** *(hidden ability)*: Ups evasion if confused.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
