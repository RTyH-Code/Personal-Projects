# #207 Gligar

*The FlyScorpion Pokémon* — **Ground / Flying**

> It usually clings to cliffs. When it spots its prey, it spreads its wings and glides down to attack.

Height 1.1 m (3′07″) · Weight 64.8 kg (142.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hyper Cutter**: Prevents Attack reduction.
- **Sand Veil**: Ups evasion in a sandstorm.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 23**: Grass / cave Lv 42 (4%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Gligar at the Four Island Day Care
