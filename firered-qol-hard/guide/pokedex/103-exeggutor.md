# #103 Exeggutor

*The Coconut Pokémon* — **Grass / Psychic**

> It is called “The Walking Tropical Rainforest.” Each of the nuts has a face and a will of its own.

Height 2 m (6′07″) · Weight 120 kg (264.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Chlorophyll**: Raises Speed in sunshine.
- **Harvest** *(hidden ability)*: May restore a used Berry.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
