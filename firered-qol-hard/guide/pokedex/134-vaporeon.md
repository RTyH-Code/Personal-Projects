# #134 Vaporeon

*The Bubble Jet Pokémon* — **Water**

> Its body's cellular structure is similar to the molecular composition of water. It can melt invisibly in water.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 29 kg (63.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Water Absorb**: Changes water into HP.
- **Hydration** *(hidden ability)*: Heals status in rain.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
