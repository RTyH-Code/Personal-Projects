# #029 Nidoran♀

*The Poison Pin Pokémon* — **Poison**

> Though small, it must be treated with caution because of its powerfully toxic barbs. The female has smaller horns.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 7 kg (15.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Poison Point**: Poisons foe on contact.
- **Rivalry**: Strong vs. the same gender.
- **Hustle** *(hidden ability)*: Trades accuracy for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 3**: Grass / cave Lv 6 (1%)
- **Safari Zone East**: Grass / cave Lv 24 (5%)
- **Safari Zone West**: Grass / cave Lv 30 (5%)
- **Underground Path North Entrance**: in-game trade for Nidoran♂ ("MS. NIDO")
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Nidoran♀ at the Four Island Day Care
