# #087 Dewgong

*The Sea Lion Pokémon* — **Water / Ice**

> Its body is covered with a pure white fur. The colder the weather, the more active it becomes.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 120 kg (264.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Thick Fat**: Heat-and-cold protection.
- **Hydration**: Heals status in rain.
- **Ice Body** *(hidden ability)*: Slight HP recovery in hail.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
