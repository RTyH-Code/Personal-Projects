# #067 Machoke

*The Superpower Pokémon* — **Fighting**

> Its formidable body never gets tired. It helps people by doing work such as the moving of heavy goods.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 70.5 kg (155.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Guts**: Ups Attack if suffering.
- **No Guard**: All moves always hit.
- **Steadfast** *(hidden ability)*: Flinching raises Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
