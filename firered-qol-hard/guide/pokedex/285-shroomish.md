# #285 Shroomish

*The Mushroom Pokémon* — **Grass**

> It prefers damp places. By day it remains still in the forest shade. It releases toxic powder from its head.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 4.5 kg (9.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Effect Spore**: Leaves spores on contact.

- **Shroomish** → [Breloom](286-breloom.md): Level 23

## Where to find it

- **Viridian Forest**: Grass / cave Lv 3 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Shroomish or Breloom at the Four Island Day Care
