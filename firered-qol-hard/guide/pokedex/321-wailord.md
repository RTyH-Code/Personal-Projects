# #321 Wailord

*The Float Whale Pokémon* — **Water**

> It is among the largest of all Pokémon. It herds prey in a pack then swallows the massed prey in one gulp.

Height 14.5 m (47′07″) · Weight 398 kg (877.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Water Veil**: Prevents burns.
- **Oblivious**: Prevents attraction.
- **Pressure** *(hidden ability)*: Raises foe's PP usage.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
