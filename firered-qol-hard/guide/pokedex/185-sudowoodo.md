# #185 Sudowoodo

*The Imitation Pokémon* — **Rock**

> It disguises itself as a tree to avoid attack. It hates water, so it will disappear if it starts raining.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 38 kg (83.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Sturdy**: Endures a KO at full HP.
- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.
- **Rattled** *(hidden ability)*: Bug, Ghost, Dark hits up Speed.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Sudowoodo does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Three Island Berry Forest**: Grass / cave Lv 31 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Sudowoodo at the Four Island Day Care
