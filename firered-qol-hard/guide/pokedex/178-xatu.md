# #178 Xatu

*The Mystic Pokémon* — **Psychic / Flying**

> In South America, it is said that its right eye sees the future and its left eye views the past.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 15 kg (33.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Synchronize**: Passes on status problems.
- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
