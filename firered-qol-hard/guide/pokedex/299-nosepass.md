# #299 Nosepass

*The Compass Pokémon* — **Rock**

> Its magnetic nose consistently faces north. Travelers check Nosepass to get their bearings.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 97 kg (213.8 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Sturdy**: Endures a KO at full HP.
- **Magnet Pull**: Traps Steel-type Pokémon.
- **Sand Force** *(hidden ability)*: Powers up moves in sand.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Nosepass does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Rock Tunnel B1F**: Rock Smash Lv 15–30 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Nosepass at the Four Island Day Care
