# #306 Aggron

*The Iron Armor Pokémon* — **Steel / Rock**

> It claims a large mountain as its sole territory. It mercilessly thrashes those that violate its space.

Height 2.1 m (6′11″) · Weight 360 kg (793.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Sturdy**: Negates 1-hit KO attacks.
- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
