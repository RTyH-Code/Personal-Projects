# #177 Natu

*The Tiny Bird Pokémon* — **Psychic / Flying**

> It usually forages for food on the ground but may, on rare occasions, hop onto branches to peck at shoots.

Height 0.2 m (0′08″) · Weight 2 kg (4.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Synchronize**: Passes on status problems.
- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.
- **Magic Bounce** *(hidden ability)*: Reflects status moves.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
