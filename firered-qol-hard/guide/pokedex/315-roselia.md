# #315 Roselia

*The Thorn Pokémon* — **Grass / Poison**

> Its flowers give off a relaxing fragrance. The stronger its aroma, the healthier the Roselia is.

Height 0.3 m (1′00″) · Weight 2 kg (4.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Natural Cure**: Heals upon switching out.
- **Poison Point**: Poisons foe on contact.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 14**: Grass / cave Lv 26 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Roselia at the Four Island Day Care
