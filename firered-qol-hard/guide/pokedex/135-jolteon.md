# #135 Jolteon

*The Lightning Pokémon* — **Electric**

> If it is angered or startled, the fur all over its body bristles like sharp needles that pierce foes.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 24.5 kg (54.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Volt Absorb**: Turns electricity into HP.
- **Quick Feet** *(hidden ability)*: Ups Speed if suffering.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
