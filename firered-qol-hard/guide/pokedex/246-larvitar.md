# #246 Larvitar

*The Rock Skin Pokémon* — **Rock / Ground**

> It is born deep underground. It can't emerge until it has entirely consumed the soil around it.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 72 kg (158.7 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Guts**: Ups Attack if suffering.
- **Sand Veil** *(hidden ability)*: Ups evasion in a sandstorm.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
