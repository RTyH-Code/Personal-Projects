# #176 Togetic

*The Happiness Pokémon* — **Fairy / Flying** *(originally Normal / Flying)*

> It grows dispirited if it is not with kind people. It can float in midair without moving its wings.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 3.2 kg (7.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hustle**: Trades accuracy for power.
- **Serene Grace**: Promotes added effects.
- **Super Luck** *(hidden ability)*: Ups critical-hit ratio.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
