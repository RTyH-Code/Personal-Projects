# #082 Magneton

*The Magnet Pokémon* — **Electric / Steel**

> A linked cluster formed of several Magnemite. It discharges powerful magnetic waves at high voltage.

Height 1 m (3′03″) · Weight 60 kg (132.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Magnet Pull**: Traps Steel-type Pokémon.
- **Sturdy**: Endures a KO at full HP.
- **Analytic** *(hidden ability)*: Ups power when moving last.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
