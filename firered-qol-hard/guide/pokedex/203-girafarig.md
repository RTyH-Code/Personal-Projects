# #203 Girafarig

*The Long Neck Pokémon* — **Normal / Psychic**

> Its tail, which also contains a small brain, may bite on its own if it notices an alluring smell.

Height 1.5 m (4′11″) · Weight 41.5 kg (91.5 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Inner Focus**: Prevents flinching.
- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.
- **Sap Sipper** *(hidden ability)*: Grass moves raise Attack.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Safari Zone East**: Grass / cave Lv 22 (4%)
- **Safari Zone North**: Grass / cave Lv 25 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Girafarig or Farigiraf at the Four Island Day Care
