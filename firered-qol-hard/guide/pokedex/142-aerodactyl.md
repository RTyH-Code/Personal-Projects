# #142 Aerodactyl

*The Fossil Pokémon* — **Rock / Flying**

> It was regenerated from a dinosaur's genetic matter that was found in amber. It flies with high-pitched cries.

Height 1.8 m (5′11″) · Weight 59 kg (130.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.
- **Pressure**: Raises foe's PP usage.
- **Unnerve** *(hidden ability)*: Foes can't eat Berries.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Evolution

Aerodactyl does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Cinnabar Island Pokémon Lab Experiment Room**: Gift, Lv 5
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Aerodactyl at the Four Island Day Care
