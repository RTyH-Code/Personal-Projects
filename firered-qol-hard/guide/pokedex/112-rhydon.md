# #112 Rhydon

*The Drill Pokémon* — **Ground / Rock**

> It begins walking on its hind legs after evolution. It can punch holes through boulders with its horn.

Height 1.9 m (6′03″) · Weight 120 kg (264.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Lightning Rod**: Absorbs Electric moves.
- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.
- **Reckless** *(hidden ability)*: Powers up recoil moves.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
