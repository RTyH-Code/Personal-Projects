# #062 Poliwrath

*The Tadpole Pokémon* — **Water / Fighting**

> An adept swimmer, it knows the front crawl, butterfly, and more. It is faster than the best human swimmers.

Height 1.3 m (4′03″) · Weight 54 kg (119.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Water Absorb**: Changes water into HP.
- **Damp**: Prevents self-destruction.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
