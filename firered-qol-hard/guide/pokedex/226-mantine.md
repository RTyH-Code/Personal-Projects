# #226 Mantine

*The Kite Pokémon* — **Water / Flying**

> Swimming freely in open seas, it may fly out of the water and over the waves if it builds up enough speed.

Height 2.1 m (6′11″) · Weight 220 kg (485.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Water Absorb**: Changes water into HP.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*

Mantine does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Route 19**: Surfing Lv 30–35 (4%)
- **Five Island Resort Gorgeous**: Surfing Lv 35–40 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Mantine at the Four Island Day Care
