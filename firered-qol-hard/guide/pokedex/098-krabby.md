# #098 Krabby

*The River Crab Pokémon* — **Water**

> It can be found near the sea. The large pincers grow back if they are torn out of their sockets.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 6.5 kg (14.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Hyper Cutter**: Prevents Attack reduction.
- **Shell Armor**: Blocks critical hits.
- **Sheer Force** *(hidden ability)*: Drops added effects for power.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
