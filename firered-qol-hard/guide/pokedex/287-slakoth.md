# #287 Slakoth

*The Slacker Pokémon* — **Normal**

> It sleeps for 20 hours every day. Making drowsy those that see it is one of its abilities.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 24 kg (52.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Truant**: Moves only every two turns.
