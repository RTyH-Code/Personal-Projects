# #191 Sunkern

*The Seed Pokémon* — **Grass**

> It lives by drinking only dewdrops from under the leaves of plants. It is said that it eats nothing else.

Height 0.3 m (1′00″) · Weight 1.8 kg (4.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Chlorophyll**: Raises Speed in sunshine.
- **Solar Power**: Ups Sp. Atk in sun, hurts.
- **Early Bird** *(hidden ability)*: Awakens quickly from sleep.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
