# #302 Sableye

*The Darkness Pokémon* — **Dark / Ghost**

> It feeds on gemstone crystals. In darkness, its eyes sparkle with the glitter of jewels.

Height 0.5 m (1′08″) · Weight 11 kg (24.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Keen Eye**: Prevents loss of accuracy.
- **Stall**: Moves after the foe.
- **Prankster** *(hidden ability)*: Status moves go first.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Five Island Lost Cave Room 1**: Grass / cave Lv 65 (5%)
- **Five Island Lost Cave Room 5**: Grass / cave Lv 65 (5%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Sableye at the Four Island Day Care
