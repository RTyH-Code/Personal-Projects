# #161 Sentret

*The Scout Pokémon* — **Normal**

> It stands on its tail so it can see a long way. If it spots an enemy, it cries loudly to warn its kind.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 6 kg (13.2 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.
- **Keen Eye**: Prevents loss of accuracy.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
