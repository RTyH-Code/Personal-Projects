# #095 Onix

*The Rock Snake Pokémon* — **Rock / Ground**

> It usually lives underground. It searches for food while boring its way through the ground at 50 miles per hour.

Height 8.8 m (28′10″) · Weight 210 kg (463.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Rock Head**: Prevents recoil damage.
- **Sturdy**: Negates 1-hit KO attacks.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
