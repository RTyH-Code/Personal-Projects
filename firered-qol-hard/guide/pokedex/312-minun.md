# #312 Minun

*The Cheering Pokémon* — **Electric**

> Its dislike of water makes it take shelter under the eaves of houses in rain. It uses pom-poms made of sparks for cheering.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 4.2 kg (9.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Minus**: Powers up with Plus/Minus.
- **Volt Absorb** *(hidden ability)*: Turns electricity into HP.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

Minun does not evolve.

## Where to find it

- **Power Plant**: Grass / cave Lv 29 (10%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Minun at the Four Island Day Care
