# #164 Noctowl

*The Owl Pokémon* — **Normal / Flying**

> When it needs to think, it rotates its head 180 degrees to sharpen its intellectual power.

Height 1.6 m (5′03″) · Weight 40.8 kg (89.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Insomnia**: Prevents sleep.
- **Keen Eye**: Prevents loss of accuracy.
- **Tinted Lens** *(hidden ability)*: Ups not very effective hits.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
