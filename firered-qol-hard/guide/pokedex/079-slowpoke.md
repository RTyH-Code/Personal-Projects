# #079 Slowpoke

*The Dopey Pokémon* — **Water / Psychic**

> It is always vacantly lost in thought, but no one knows what it is thinking about. It is good at fishing with its tail.

Height 1.2 m (3′11″) · Weight 36 kg (79.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Oblivious**: Prevents attraction.
- **Own Tempo**: Prevents confusion.
- **Regenerator** *(hidden ability)*: Heals when switched out.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*

## Where to find it

- **Route 12**: Surfing Lv 20–30 (5%)
- **Seafoam Islands 1F**: Grass / cave Lv 33 (4%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Slowpoke or Slowbro or Slowking at the Four Island Day Care
