# #118 Goldeen

*The Goldfish Pokémon* — **Water**

> Its dorsal and pectoral fins are strongly developed like muscles. It can swim at a speed of five knots.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 15 kg (33.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.
- **Water Veil**: Prevents burns.
- **Lightning Rod** *(hidden ability)*: Absorbs Electric moves.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
