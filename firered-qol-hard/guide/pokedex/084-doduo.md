# #084 Doduo

*The Twin Bird Pokémon* — **Normal / Flying**

> A two-headed Pokémon that was discovered as a sudden mutation. It runs at a pace of over 60 miles per hour.

Height 1.4 m (4′07″) · Weight 39.2 kg (86.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.
- **Early Bird**: Awakens quickly from sleep.

*Each individual has one of the two. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can swap it.*
