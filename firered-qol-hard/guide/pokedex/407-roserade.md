# #407 Roserade

*The Bouquet Pokémon* — **Grass / Poison**

> With the movements of a dancer, it strikes with whips that are densely lined with poison thorns.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 14.5 kg (32.0 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Natural Cure**: Heals upon switching out.
- **Poison Point**: Poisons foe on contact.
- **Technician** *(hidden ability)*: Powers up weak moves.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
