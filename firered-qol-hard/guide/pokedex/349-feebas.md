# #349 Feebas

*The Fish Pokémon* — **Water**

> Ridiculed for its shabby appearance, it is ignored by researchers. It lives in ponds choked with weeds.

Height 0.6 m (2′00″) · Weight 7.4 kg (16.3 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Raises Speed in rain.

- **Feebas** → [Milotic](350-milotic.md): Use a Deep Sea Scale *(originally: Level up with high Beauty)*

## Where to find it

- **Safari Zone North**: Super Rod Lv 15–35 (4%)
- **Five Island Water Labyrinth**: Super Rod Lv 15–25 (40%)
- Breeding: hatches from an Egg left by a Feebas or Milotic at the Four Island Day Care
