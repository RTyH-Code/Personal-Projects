# #088 Grimer

*The Sludge Pokémon* — **Poison**

> Sludge exposed to X rays from the moon transformed into Grimer. It loves feeding on filthy things.

Height 0.9 m (2′11″) · Weight 30 kg (66.1 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Stench**: May make foes flinch.
- **Sticky Hold**: Prevents item theft.
- **Poison Touch** *(hidden ability)*: May poison on contact.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
