# #078 Rapidash

*The Fire Horse Pokémon* — **Fire**

> It can gallop at a top speed of 150 miles per hour. It can race as fast as a bullet train while ablaze.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 95 kg (209.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Run Away**: Makes escaping easier.
- **Flash Fire**: Powers up if hit by fire.
- **Flame Body** *(hidden ability)*: Burns the foe on contact.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
