# #317 Swalot

*The Poison Bag Pokémon* — **Poison**

> It can swallow a tire whole in one gulp. It secretes a horribly toxic fluid from the pores on its body.

Height 1.7 m (5′07″) · Weight 80 kg (176.4 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Liquid Ooze**: Draining causes injury.
- **Sticky Hold**: Prevents item theft.

*Each individual has one of the two, set by its personality value.*
