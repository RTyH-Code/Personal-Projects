# #263 Zigzagoon

*The Tiny Raccoon Pokémon* — **Normal**

> A Pokémon with abundant curiosity. It shows an interest in everything, so it always zigs and zags.

Height 0.4 m (1′04″) · Weight 17.5 kg (38.6 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Pickup**: May pick up items.
- **Gluttony**: Eats pinch Berries early.
- **Quick Feet** *(hidden ability)*: Ups Speed if suffering.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
