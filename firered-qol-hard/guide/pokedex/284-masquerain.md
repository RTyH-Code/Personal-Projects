# #284 Masquerain

*The Eyeball Pokémon* — **Bug / Flying**

> The antennae have distinctive patterns that look like eyes. When it rains, they grow heavy, making flight impossible.

Height 0.8 m (2′07″) · Weight 3.6 kg (7.9 lbs)

## Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the foe's Attack.
- **Unnerve** *(hidden ability)*: Foes can't eat Berries.

*Wild Pokémon have one of the regular abilities. After you become Champion, the coach in the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center can switch it to any ability listed here, including the hidden one.*
